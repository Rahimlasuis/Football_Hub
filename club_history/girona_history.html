Our Club's Golden History

1930

The Genesis

Girona Football Club was founded on July 23, 1930 at the Norat Café in Girona, Spain. Created to give the city a strong representative football team after previous local clubs folded.

1970

Stadium Opening

Moved permanently to the Estadi Montilivi in 1970 after decades at the older Vista Alegre pitch.

1980-2000

Lower Tier

Girona spent most of the late 20th century bouncing between spain,s third, fourth, and even fifth regional tiers. It wasn't some of their best years

2008

Promotion

Finally Won promotion back to the Spanish Second Division in 2008 following a 49-year absence from that level.

2017

Laliga Promotion

Finally for the first time in history Laliga secured promotion to the top flight for the first time in club history in 2017.

2017

City Football Group

Joined the City Football Group in 2017, acquiring a co-ownership stake that aligned them with clubs like Manchester City.

2023-24

Campions League

Finished near the top in the Laliga Leauge and qualified for the UEFA Champions League for the first time in history.
