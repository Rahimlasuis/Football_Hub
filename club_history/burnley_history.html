Burnley

Take a journey with us through our legendary unforgettable eras

Our Club's Golden History

1882

The Genesis

Our Legendary club was oficially founded. Burnley Football Club is one of the oldest professional soccer teams in England. Started as a rugby club named Burnley Rovers before switching to association football.

1888

The Football League

Became one of the 12 original Football League founders.

1914

Early Glory

Won their first major trophy, the FA Cup, beating Liverpool 1–0.

1921

The Record

Crowned champions of England for the first time, setting a 30-match unbeaten record that stood for decades.

1959/60

Early Success

Won their second First Division title under manager Harry Potts, relying heavily on a stellar youth academy. Making Burnley one of the smallest towns to ever claim an English top-tier championship

1987

The Close call

Narrowly avoided dropping out of the Football League entirely on a dramatic final day.

1992

The Historic Achievement

Became only the second club to win all four professional English divisions.

2010s-2020s

Sean Dyche

Enjoyed a long stable spell under Sean Dyche, reaching European competition qualification alongside regular Premier League campaigns
