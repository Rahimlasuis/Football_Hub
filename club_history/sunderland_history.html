Our Club's Golden History

1879

The Genesis

Founded in October 1879 by Scottish schoolmaster James Allan, Sunderland Association Football Club grew into an early English powerhouse.

1890

The Football League

Started as a teachers' team before opening membership to the public. Joined the Football League in 1890.

1980s

First Glories

Won three First Division titles in the 1890s (1890–91, 1892–93, 1894–95)

1990s

More Success

Sunderland added more First Division League titles in 1902, 1913,1936

1937

FA Cup

Sunderland claimed their first FA Cup in 1937

1973

More Glory

Stunned Leeds United 1–0 in the FA Cup final while playing in the Second Division.

1997

The Parking

Left Roker Park after 99 years to open the Stadium of Light in 1997.
