Our Club's Golden History

1878

The Genesis

Formed at St. Domingo Methodist Church in Breckfield Road North, Liverpool, so members could play sports year-round.

1979

The Name Change

Renamed Everton FC after the local district.

1891

Anfield Era

Moved to Anfield, their home ground. Played at Anfield initially, winning their first League Championship in 1891.

1892

The Move

Left Anfield due to a rent dispute with the landowner, moving across Stanley Park to build Goodison Park—the first purpose-built football ground in England

1927/28

Dixie Dean

Set an all-time English record by scoring 60 league goals in the 1927–28 title-winning season.

1980s

Howard Kendall's

Managed by Howard Kendall, this decade was the club's most successful modern spell, yielding two league titles (1985, 1987), an FA Cup (1984), and the European Cup Winners' Cup in 1985.

1995

Recent Trophies

Won their most recent major trophy, the FA Cup, by defeating Manchester United 1–0 under Joe Royle.
