Take a journey with us through our legendary unforgettable eras

Our Club's Golden History

1904

The Predecessors

Leeds City is formed, but wartime financial irregularities lead to its expulsion from the league.

1919

The Genesis

Leeds United was founded in 1919 after its predecessor, Leeds City, was forced to shut down by the Football League due to illegal player payments

1920s-1050s

Good amd Bad Days

The club spends decades bouncing between the First and Second Divisions of English football

1960

Don Revie

Don Revie becomes manager and changes the team kit to all-white to mirror Real Madrid.

1968

First Trophy

Leeds United wins their first major trophy. The League Cup and an Inter-cities Fair Cup

1969

First English League

Leeds Unied won their first English League First Division title.

1972

Victory

Leeds United wins their first FA Cup Victory in 1972.

1974

More Success

Leeds Unied wins their second English League First Division Title

1988

Howard Wilkinson

Howard Wilkinson takes over as manager and steers the club back to the top flight.

1992

Last Division Championship

Leeds wins the final First Division championship before it is rebranded as the Premier League.

2001

UEFA Champions League

A young squad led by David O'Leary reaches the semi-finals of the UEFA Champions League.

2004

Financial Crisis

Massive debts catch up to the club after high-risk spending, resulting in Premier League relegation and severe financial crisis.

2007-2010

Relegation

The club drops even lower, spending several seasons in League One (the third tier) for the first time in history.

2020

The Return

Managed by Marcelo Bielsa, Leeds wins the EFL Championship and returns to the Premier League after a 16-year absence.

Present

Current Struggle

The club continues fighting to cement its long-term status in English elite football.
