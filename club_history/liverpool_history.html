Our Club's Golden History

1892

The Genesis

Our Legendary club was founded in 1892 by John Houlding following a boardroom dispute at Everton. Renowned for their passionate fanbase and "You'll Never Walk Alone" anthem.

1953/54

Relegation

After 49 uninterrupted years in the top division, the club was relegated to the Second Division after a disappointing season.

1962

Promotion

Liverpool got promoted back to the top division

1964

Victory

Liverpool won the top league after 17 years.

1965

FA Cup

Liverpool won its very first FA Cup

1983

Paisley/Joe Fagan

Paisley retired in 1983 and was replaced by his assistant, Joe Fagan.[16] Liverpool won the League, League Cup and European Cup in Fagan's first season, becoming the first English side to win three trophies in a season

1985

The Fight

Just before a match against juventus conflict arised among fans and 39 players were banned causing the English club 7 years ban and convictions of some fourteen liverpool fan for manslaughter

1988/89

The thin Margin

Liverpool was involved in the closest finish to a league season during the 1988–89 season, finishing equal with Arsenal on both points and goal difference, but lost the title on total goals scored when Arsenal scored the final goal in the last minute of the season

2024

Coaches

Arne Slot was announced as Klopp's successor in May 2024,[59] and the club won a record-equalling twentieth top-flight league title in his first season in charge.[60] After a fifth place finish in the league the following season, Slot was sacked as head coach on 30 May 2026,[61] and was replaced by Andoni Iraola five days later.
