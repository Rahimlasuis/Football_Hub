Bayern Munich

Take a journey with us through our legendary unforgettable eras

Our Club's Golden History

1900

The Genesis

Founded in 1900 by Franz John and some other players, FC Bayern Munich grew from a local breakaway group into Germany's most successful football club. Seventeen men met at Café Gisela to establish the club.

1932

The First Trophy

Bayern won their first German championship by defeating Eintracht Frankfurt.

1960

World War II

The club suffered under the Nazi regime due to its Jewish roots. President Kurt Landauer fled into exile.

1963

The Crisis

Bayern was left out of the newly formed Bundesliga due to financial troubles.

1965

The Rebirth

Bayern made a remarkable comeback, promoted to the Bundesliga led by young stars Franz Beckenbauer, Gerd Müller, and Sepp Maier.

1974

Continental Trophy

Won the European Cup Winners' Cup for their first continental trophy.

1974-1976

The European Glory

Won three consecutive European Cups in 1974, 1975, and 1976 to cement an international dynasty.

1987

Prominence

Bayern Munich became undoubtfully the best, most prominent and most successful club in germany. They became the record domestic champions of germany.

2001

Champions League

Bayern Munich won their first UEFA Champions League title.

2013

Another

Bayern Munich won another UEFA Champions League title. The 2013 victory completed a historic treble (Bundesliga, DFB-Pokal, and Champions League).

2020

Continental Treble

Secured another historic continental treble by winning their sixth Champions League title.

2023

Recent Years

Continued domestic dominance, setting a record of 11 consecutive Bundesliga titles through 2023.
