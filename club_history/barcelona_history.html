Our Club's Golden History

1899

The Genesis

Our Legendary club was oficially founded by Swiss businessman Joan Gamper, FC Barcelona has grown into one of the world's most decorated football clubs, with a rich cultural identity

1928/29

The First Golden Era

The club won the La Liga championship in the 1928-29

1936-70

Turmoil and Hardships

During the Spanish Civil War and the subsequent dictatorship of Francisco Franco, Barcelona faced severe hardships. Club president Josep Sunyol was assassinated by Franco's troops

1970-90

The Dream Team

This era marked the arrival of the dutch superstar Johan Cruyff. A creative, fluid, and attacking style. Returning as manager in the late 1980s, Cruyff built the legendary "Dream Team," featuring players like Pep Guardiola, Hristo Stoichkov, and Ronald Koeman.

----

Lionel Messi

In the 21st century, Barcelona established itself as a dominant force in world football, bolstered by its famed youth academy, La Masia. Under manager Frank Rijkaard and later the legendary Pep Guardiola, the club adopted the famous tiki-taka passing style. With global icons like Lionel Messi, Ronaldinho, Xavi, and Andrés Iniesta

2009

The Treble

Barça secured multiple Champions League titles and became the first Spanish team to achieve the continental "treble" in 2009.

Success

Today, FC Barcelona continues to compete at the pinnacle of global football, relying on its storied history and commitment to fluid, attacking play
