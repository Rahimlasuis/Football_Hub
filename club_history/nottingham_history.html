Our Club's Golden History

1865

The Genesis

Founded in 1865, Nottingham Forest F.C. is one of England's oldest professional football clubs Shinty players met at the Clinton Arms in 1865 and chose to form a football club instead.

1886

Red

Adopted "Garibaldi Red" shirts named after Italian patriot Giuseppe Garibaldi. Helped other famous clubs, like Arsenal in 1886, by donating red kits.

1898

First Trophy

Won their first major trophy by defeating Derby County 3–1 in the 1898 FA Cup Final.

1959

FA Cup

Claimed a quiet FA Cup victory in 1959, but otherwise stayed away from major domestic threats for decades.

1975

Brian Clough

Brian Clough took charge in 1975 and turned the struggling second-tier club into champions.

1977-78

Division League

Won the First Division league title in the 1977–78 season straight after promotion.

1979-80

European Glory

Lifted consecutive European Cups in 1979 and 1980, an incredible peak of international success.

1993

Retirement

Captured League Cups in 1978 and 1989 before Clough retired in 1993.

1993

The Dropback

Suffered a decline after Clough left, leading to relegation drops, including a stint in the third tier.

2022

Promotion

Fought through years in the Championship before winning promotion back to the Premier League via the 2022 play-offs.
