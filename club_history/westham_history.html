Take a journey with us through our legendary unforgettable eras

Our Club's Golden History

1885

The Genesis

West Ham United was founded in June 1895 as Thames Ironworks F.C. by foreman Dave Taylor and shipbuilder owner Arnold Hills. Started as a works team for the Thames Ironworks and Shipbuilding Company in Canning Town, East London.

1897

Crest and Colors

The crossed rivet hammers on the badge honor the shipyard workers. The team later adopted claret and blue kits.

1900

Reformation

Reformed as West Ham United after the original works team dissolved.

1904

The Boleyn Ground

Moved to Upton Park, playing there for 112 years.

1919

FootBall League

Joined the Football League Second Division after World War I.

1964/1965

Glory

Managed by Ron Greenwood, the club won the FA Cup in 1964 and the European Cup Winners' Cup in 1965.

1966

World Cup Glory

West Ham legends Bobby Moore, Geoff Hurst, and Martin Peters played vital roles in England's 1966 World Cup win.

1975/1980

More FA Cups

Added more FA Cup titles in 1975 (under John Lyall) and 1980, when they beat Arsenal while still playing in the Second Division.

2016

The Move

Left the historic Boleyn Ground to move into the Olympic Stadium in Stratford

2023

Recent Success

Won the UEFA Europa Conference League in 2023 under David Moyes, claiming their first major European trophy in decades.
