Our Club's Golden History

1874

The Genesis

Aston Villa FC, founded in 1874, is one of England’s oldest and most successful football clubs, instrumental in shaping modern football and the Football League.

1886

Scotsman George Ramsay

By the late 1880s, Villa had become a dominant force in English football, pioneering a sophisticated passing style under Scotsman George Ramsay, who became the world’s first professional football manager in 1886

1888

The Preofessional League

Aston Villa played a pivotal role in the professionalization of football. Club director William McGregor founded the Football League in 1888, with Villa as one of the twelve founding members

1890-1970

The Golden Era

Villa’s early dominance included multiple First Division titles and FA Cups before the 20th century. Under George Ramsay, the club won six League Championships and six FA Cups, making it England’s most successful club

1930

1935

Relegation

After the 1930s, Villa experienced a decline, including its first relegation in 1935–36 after 48 years in the top flight

1980/81/82

Ron Saunders

Under Ron Saunders in the 1970s, Villa returned to the top tier, winning the First Division title in 1980–81.The club achieved European glory by winning the European Cup in 1981–82 and the European Super Cup in 1982

1992

The Premier League

Aston Villa was a founding member of the Premier League in 1992, one of only three clubs to have been founding members of both the Football League and the Premier League
