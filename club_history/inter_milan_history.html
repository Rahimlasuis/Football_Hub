Our Club's Golden History

1908

The Genesis

Inter Milan was founded on March 9, 1908, by a breakaway group of 44 members from the Milan Cricket and Football Club (now AC Milan). The founders wated o welcome forign players which earned them the nickname 'brother of the world'

1910

Early years

Inter's origins lie in a split from AC Milan due to a ban on signing foreign players. The new club adopted the name Internazionale to reflect its open, global philosophy. Success was swift, as they won their first cup (Italian championship) in 1910.

1930s

Giuseppe Meazza

During the 1930s, the team was propelled by club legend Giuseppe Meazza, who scored 284 goals and remains the club's all-time leading scorer.

----

San Siro

The club's iconic home ground, the San Siro Stadium, was built by AC Milan in 1926 and has been shared by both Milan clubs since 1947. It was later officially renamed the Stadio Giuseppe Meazza

1960s

The Golden Age

In the 1960s, under visionary manager Helenio Herrera, Inter experienced its first true golden age, known as La Grande Inter. Herrera revolutionized tactics with his catenaccio (door-bolt) defensive system. This era brought three Serie A titles, two consecutive European Cups (1964 and 1965), and two Intercontinental Cups

2006-10

The Golden Era

After decades of moderate success and occasional domestic struggles in the late 20th century, the club enjoyed a massive resurgence in the late 2000s, winning five consecutive Serie A titles between 2006 and 2010.

2010

The 2010 Treble

The peak of this era came under manager José Mourinho, when Inter became the first—and only—Italian club to achieve the historic treble of the Serie A title, Coppa Italia, and Champions League in a single season

2020s

Modern Success

After a period of transition in the 2010s, the club re-established itself as a dominant force in Italian football, winning additional league titles and multiple Coppa Italia and Supercoppa Italiana trophies.
