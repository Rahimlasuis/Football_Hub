Our Club's Golden History

1901

The Genesis

Founded on December 19, 1909, by young football players in Dortmund, eighteen young men formed the club after breaking away from a strict local church youth group. The name "Borussia" came from a local Borussia brewery in Dortmund.

1913

Colours

The team swapped their original blue and white for the famous black and yellow in 1913.

1956

First Campionship

Dortmund won their first national titles in 1956 and 1957,

1963

Pre-Bundesliga Championship

Borusia Dortmund lifted the pre-Bundesliga Championship in 1963.

1966

European Pioneers

: In 1966, they beat Liverpool to win the European Cup Winners' Cup, becoming the first German club to lift a European trophy.

1995

Bundesliga Returns

Dortmund under the management of manager Ottmar Hitzfeld, won back to back Bundesliga titles in 1995 and 1996.

1997

Champions League

They shocked Europe in 1997 by defeating Juventus 3–1 to win the UEFA Champions League, then captured the Intercontinental Cup

2000s

Financial Crisis

Due to Overexpansion, Heavy and unstructured spending led the club to a near bankruptcy in the early 2000s before a painful restructuring saved them.

2008

Jurgen Klopp

Appointed in 2008, Klopp built an energetic young team that won back-to-back Bundesliga titles in 2011

2012

The Double

After the 2011 bundesliga title dortmund also won another bundesliga title in 2012 and also secured a domestic double in 2012

2013/2024

Modern Era

BVB reached the Champions League final in 2013 and 2024, remaining a dominant force in German football and renowned globally for developing elite young talent in front of their famous "Yellow Wall".
