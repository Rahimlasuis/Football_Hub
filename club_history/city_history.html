Our Club's Golden History

1880

The Genesis

Our Legendary club was oficially founded. It was ffounded in 1880 as St. Mark’s (West Gorton), has evolved from a local church team into one of the most successful football clubs in the world. The club officially became Manchester City in 1894 and plays its home matches at the Etihad Stadium.

1904

The First Major Trophy

City won their first major title by defeating Bolton Wanderers in the FA Cup, making them the first major club in Manchester to win a major honour.

1969

Trophies

Manchester City Won their First FA Cup and first European Trophy

1970

Trophies

They won the European Trophy and Cup Winners Cup

1998

Dark ages

The late 70s ushered in an inconsistent era for the club, marked by frequent managerial changes, financial trouble, and relegations—including dropping to the third tier of English football in 1998.

2002

Return to Top

After a lot of Hardwork, Manchester City Won their Promotion back to the Premier league, laying the groundwork for future investment

2003

Stadium Move

The club left Maine Road (their home since 1923) and relocated to the City of Manchester Stadium (now the Etihad Stadium), built for the 2002 Commonwealth Games.

2008

The Purchase

In 2008, a massive shift occurred when the club was purchased by the Abu Dhabi United Group, led by Sheikh Mansour bin Zayed Al Nahyan. This investment transformed Manchester City into a global powerhouse.

2012

Premier League Title

In 2012, Sergio Agüero scored his famous, dramatic last-minute goal against Queens Park Rangers, securing City's first league title in 44 years.

----

The Pep Guardiolla Revolution

Under manager Pep Guardiola, the club achieved unprecedented domestic and international success. Guardiola led City to four consecutive Premier League titles, the first team in English football history to achieve this feat
