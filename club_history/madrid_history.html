Real

In 1920, King Alfonso XIII bestowed the honorific title "Real" (meaning Royal) upon the club.

1939

First League

In 1929, Real Madrid became a founding member of La Liga, Spain's top national division, and won its first league title in 1932

1950

Bernabeu

The club's modern identity was forged by legendary president Santiago Bernabéu, who took over in 1943. Bernabéu oversaw the construction of the iconic stadium that bears his name today and orchestrated the signing stars like Di Stefano, Ferenc Puskás, and Francisco Gento

1956-60

European Dominance

Real Madrid dominated the football league during these five years of leendary football. Real Madrid completely dominated the newly formed European Cup, winning the first five consecutive tournaments from 1956 to 1960

1960s

The European Dominance

After their 1950s dynasty, Real Madrid transitioned to dominating the domestic league, winning eight La Liga titles in the 1960s alone

1980s

Emilio and La Quinta

In the 1980s, the club relied on "La Quinta del Buitre," a highly successful group of homegrown players led by Emilio Butragueño, which secured five consecutive league titles

1998

One out of many

While domestic success continued, the club endured a 32-year drought in Europe until they claimed their seventh European Cup in 1998

2000

Florentino Perez

In 2000, Florentino Pérez was elected club president and introduced the "Galácticos" policy, setting a precedent of signing the world's most expensive and talented players—including Luís Figo, Zinedine Zidane, Ronaldo, and David Beckham.

2009

The Goat CR7

After 2009, Pérez returned and built a new dynasty spearheaded by Cristiano Ronaldo, who became the club's all-time leading scorer
