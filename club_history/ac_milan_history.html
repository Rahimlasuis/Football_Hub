Our Club's Golden History

1899

The Genesis

Our Legendary club was oficially founded. It was founded on december 16 by Englishmen Herbert Kilpin and Alfred Edwards

1901

The First Achievement

Originall founded as Milan Foot-Ball and Cricket Club, the squad quickly achieved domestic success, winning their first league in 1901 and the subsequently in 1906 and 1907

1908

The Dispute

A dispute within the club over signing foreign players in 1908 led to a splinter group breaking away to form their eternal rivals Inter Milan. Matches between these two are known as Derby della Madonnina

1986

The Purchase

The Club was sold to media mogul Silvio Berlusconi. Under his ownership, and guided by innovative managers like Arrigo Sacchi, the team assembled legendary squads featuring Dutch icons like Marco van Basten, Ruud Gullit, and Frank Rijkaard.

2000s

In the 1990s and 2000s, under managers Fabio Capello and Carlo Ancelotti, Milan continued to dominate, winning multiple Champions League trophies

----

San Siro

AC Milan plays its home fixtures at the iconic San Siro stadium, a venue they have shared with Inter Milan since 1947. The club is famous for producing and harboring legends of the sport, most notably Paolo Maldini and Franco Baresi
