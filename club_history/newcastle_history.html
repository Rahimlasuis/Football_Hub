Our Club's Golden History

1892

The Genesis

Founded in 1892 in Newcastle upon Tyne, Newcastle United Football Club emerged from a merger of local teams and moved into St. James' Park.

1905

The First Trophy

Newcastle won its first Division League title, marking the beginning of a golden era for the club.

1907/1908

More Success

Newcastle won their second and third First Division League title in 1907 and 1908 respectively

1910

FA Cup

Newcastle won their first FA Cup in 1910

1924

FA Cup

Newcastle won their second FA Cup in 1924

1950s

Jackie Milburn

Newcastle continued to achieve success and lifted three more additional FA Cups in the years 1951, 1953 and 1955 respectively led by the legendary forward Jackie Milburn

1969

European Honors

Claimed their first major European honor by winning the Inter-Cities Fairs Cup in 1969.

1990s

Kevin Keegan & Alan Shearer

Managed by Kevin Keegan in the mid-1990s, the team played thrilling attacking football, finishing as Premier League runners-up twice with star striker Alan Shearer.

2000s

Bobby Robson

Reached the UEFA Champions League under Sir Bobby Robson in the early 2000s

2021

New Ownership

A Saudi-led consortium completed a high-profile takeover of the club in October 2021.

2025

Eddie Howe

Returned to the UEFA Champions League and captured the Carabao Cup in 2025 under Eddie Howe, marking their first major piece of silverware in seven decades
