Our Club's Golden History

1889

The Genesis

Brentford Football Club is a professional English football team based in West London. Founded in October 10, 1889, the club is nicknamed "The Bees"

1904

Griffin Park

Moved into Griffin Park, a famous ground featuring a pub on each of its four corners.

1920

The Big move

Brentford made the big move of joining the Football League in the new Third Division South.

1926-1939

The first Golden Era

Under manager Harry Curtis, the team achieved a golden era, winning the Second Division in 1934–35 and finishing 5th in the top-flight First Division in 1935–36

1947-1962

The Relegation

Relegated from the top tier after World War II, beginning a steady slide down into the Fourth Division by 1962.

1967

The Merge

Survived a near-death financial crisis and a blocked attempt to merge with Queens Park Rangers.

1970s-1990s

Good & Bad days

Spent years bouncing between the third and fourth tiers, famous occasionally as cup giant-killers—such as knocking Manchester City out of the FA Cup in 1989

2012

Mathew Benham

Taken over by lifelong fan and professional gambler Matthew Benham, whose data-driven recruitment model transformed the club's fortunes

2020

Gtech Stadium

Played their final match at Griffin Park before moving to the new Gtech Community Stadium

2021

The Promotion

Won the EFL Championship play-off final under Thomas Frank, earning promotion to the Premier League for the very first time in their history.
