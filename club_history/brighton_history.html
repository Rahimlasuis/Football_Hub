Our Club's Golden History

1901

The Genesis

Our Legendary club was oficially founded. A small group of local foorball players gathered to become a global football club Brighton and Hove Albion F.C.

1910

First Victory

Won the FA Charity Shield in 1910 by beating Aston Villa.

1920

The Early years

They joined the Football Lague Third Division and startd their professional career in 1920

1979

The Peak

Brighton reached the pinnacle of their success in 1979, by reaching thr old first division for the first time

1983

The Final

Reached the FA Cup Final in 1983, losing to Manchester United after a replay.

1983/1990s

Relegation

Relegated from the top flight in 1983, followed by a sharp financial decline that dropped them to the bottom tier of the Football League by the 1990s

1997

The Trade

Sold their historic Goldstone Ground in 1997, leaving the club homeless and close to exiting the Football League entirely.

1999

The Return

Played home matches away in Gillingham for two years, then returned to Brighton at a small athletics facility called Withdean Stadium from 1999 to 2011

2009

Tony Bloom

Lifelong fan and poker millionaire Tony Bloom became chairman in 2009, investing heavily in the club.

2011

The New Era

Move to the Amex Stadium (also known as the Falmer Stadium) in 2011, marking the beginning of a new chapter for the club.

2020

The Modern Era

Enter the modern era with significant investments and a renewed focus on success.

2023

Brighton in the Premier League

Brighton and Hove Albion F.C. continues to compete in the Premier League, striving for success and building on their rich history.

2023

European Contnders

Qualified for the UEFA Europa League in 2023 after finishing sixth in the Premier League, marking their first-ever European campaign
