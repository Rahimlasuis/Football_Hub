Our Club's Golden History

1886

The Genesis

Our Legendary club was founded by munitions workers at the royal Arsenal in South-east London

1893

Football League Pioneer

They became the first club in Southern English club to join thr Football League

1910

Financial Ruin

Due to financial ruin, the club was purchased by businessman Henry Noris

1913

Highbury Stadium

The club moved to the Highbury Stadium in North London, which would be their home for the next 93 years

----

The Guners

The famous moniker and cannon emblem stem from their original roots as a munitions factory team

1925

Herbert Chapman

Appointed Manager, Chapman revolutionized thr club tactics, kit, introducing the iconic red with white sleeve and stadium

1930

SilverWare

Under Chapman watch the team won their first major silverware--FA Cup and later continue to dominate the English Football

1996

Arrival of Wenger

Appointed in1996, the Frenchman revolutinized the club's playing stylee, diet and scouting

2003-04

The Invincibles

Arsenal achieved the impossible by going undefeated throughout the entire 38-game Premier League season. To celebrate, the club was awarded a unique, gold version of the Premier League trophy

2006

Stadium Move

Arsenal bid farewell to Highbury and moved to the modern, 60,000-seat Emirates Stadium to secure long-term financial growth

2018...

Rebuilding Phase

Following Wenger's departure, the club navigated managerial transitions with Unai Emery before club legend Mikel Arteta took charge on December 2019.

2026

Premier league

After over twenty years Arsenal finally won the English Premier League and lost in the Champions league finals to Paris Saint German. This match was decided with Penalties
