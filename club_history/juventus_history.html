Our Club's Golden History

1897

The Genesis

Juventus was founded in November 1897 by students from the Massimo d'Azeglio Lyceum in Turin. Formed as Sport-Club Juventus by local youths; initially wore pink shirts.

1903

Black and White

Adopted the iconic black-and-white striped shirts, inspired by English club Notts County.

1905

The First

Juventus won their very first italian Championship in 1905.

1923

The Sales

The Agnelli family (owners of Fiat) took control of juventus in 1923, providing long-term financial stability.

1930s

The Golden Era

Secured five consecutive league titles (Quinquennio d'Oro) during a major golden era.

1950s-1970s

Legends

Dominated Italian football with legendary players like Giampiero Boniperti, John Charles, and Omar Sívori.

1977

European Trophy

Juventus Captured their very first European trophy, the UEFA Cup in 1977.

1985

European Glory

Won their first European Cup (Champions League) in a final tragically overshadowed by the Heysel Stadium disaster.

1996

Coach Marcello Lippi

Claimed a second Champions League title under coach Marcello Lippi, defeating Ajax on penalties

2006

Relegation

Relegated to Serie B following the Calciopoli match-fixing scandal, which stripped them of two recent titles.

2007

Promotion

Juventus immediately bounced back to the italian top League the following year.

2012-2020

Modern Era

Set a historic milestone by winning nine consecutive Serie A titles (Scudetti), anchored by a staunch defense and stars like Gianluigi Buffon, Andrea Pirlo, and Cristiano Ronaldo.
